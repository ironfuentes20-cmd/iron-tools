Paste your list of people with indicators and it tells you who to attend to first, and why.

DemonstrationThe model is reasonable, but its weights and thresholds are not validated against external evidence. Use it to structure the conversation, not to decide on its own.

The tool itself is still in Spanish. Its rules and error messages come from the specification packages, which are written in Spanish and are the source of truth; the interface around them is being translated tool by tool.

Ordena a quién atender primero según los factores y pesos que tú declares. El ejemplo es rotación de personal, pero el mismo motor sirve para deserción estudiantil o cartera: cambias los factores y ya está.

Sin respaldo

No se declaró un informe de respaldo con cobertura, falsos positivos y negativos, ni versión de datos. Sin eso, esta priorización no debería sustentar una decisión sobre personas.
